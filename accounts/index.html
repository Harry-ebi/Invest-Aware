Accounts & Wrappers

One idea underpins this whole section: what you own and where you hold it are two different things. Get that clear and the rest falls into place.

The key idea

A wrapper is the account. Your investments are what you put inside it. The same fund could sit inside an ISA, a pension or a general account — the investment could be identical, but the tax treatment and access rules can be very different.
