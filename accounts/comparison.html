Comparing accounts & wrappers

A high-level map of how the main wrappers differ. The purpose is understanding — not choosing.

Each of these accounts can hold investments, but they differ in their tax treatment, their limits and the rules about getting at your money. This table is a simplified overview to show the shape of the differences.

How to read this

The point isn't to find the “best” column. Each wrapper is a different tool: an ISA for flexible, tax-efficient investing; a GIA for investing beyond wrapper limits; a pension for long-term retirement saving with employer contributions and tax relief but restricted access; a LISA for a first home or later life with a bonus and a withdrawal charge. What suits a person depends on their goals, timeframe and circumstances.

Understanding, not choosing

InvestAware doesn't tell you which wrapper to use — that's a personal decision, and for many people one worth taking regulated advice on. This page exists so you can understand the differences well enough to ask better questions.
