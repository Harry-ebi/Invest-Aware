Comparing accounts & wrappers

A high-level map of how the main wrappers differ. The purpose is understanding — not choosing.

These aren't competing products to rank in a league table — they're different tools for different jobs. This overview compares them across the things that actually differ, so you can see which suits a particular purpose.

How to read this

The point isn't to find the “best” column. Each wrapper is a different tool: an ISA for flexible, tax-efficient investing; a GIA as a flexible taxable investment account with no specific contribution limit; a pension for long-term retirement saving with employer contributions and tax relief but restricted access; a LISA for a first home or later life with a bonus and a withdrawal charge. What suits a person depends on their goals, timeframe and circumstances.

Understanding, not choosing

InvestAware doesn't tell you which wrapper to use — that's a personal decision, and for many people one worth taking regulated advice on. This page exists so you can understand the differences well enough to ask better questions.
