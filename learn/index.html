How investments work

The building blocks. Once you know what shares, bonds, funds, ETFs and indexes actually are, most investing language stops being intimidating.

01

Shares

Owning a small part of a company — how shares can produce returns, and why individual shares carry real risk.

 02

Bonds

Lending to a government or company in return for interest and repayment. Credit risk and interest-rate risk.

 03

Funds

Pooling your money with others to gain exposure to many investments at once — a common way to spread across many holdings.

 04

ETFs

A fund that trades on an exchange. Why “ETF” does not automatically mean “passive”.

 05

Indexes

A defined way of measuring a market — like the FTSE 100 or S&P 500 — and what an index fund tries to do.
