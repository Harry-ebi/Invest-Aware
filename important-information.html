Important information

Please read this alongside anything else on the site. It sets out what InvestAware is, and what it isn't.

Education, not advice

InvestAware provides financial education and general information only. Nothing on this website constitutes personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any investment or financial product.

Investments can fall as well as rise in value and you may get back less than you invest. Tax rules and allowances can change, and their impact depends on individual circumstances.

General information only

The content here is designed to help you understand how investing, pensions and long-term financial planning work in general terms. It does not take account of your personal circumstances, goals or financial situation. Because of that, it cannot tell you what is right for you. If you need advice tailored to your situation, consider speaking to a suitably qualified, regulated financial adviser.

Investment risk

The value of investments, and any income from them, can go down as well as up. You may get back less than you invested. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future results, and figures used on this site to illustrate concepts — such as growth curves and the effect of costs — are simplified illustrations, not forecasts or promises of any particular outcome.

Illustrations and assumptions

Where charts or examples appear, they use round, deliberately simple assumptions chosen to explain a concept clearly. Real returns vary from year to year, are not guaranteed and can be negative. Real inflation, costs and tax also vary. Illustrations should be read as teaching aids, not as expectations for real investments.

Tax, allowances and rules

References to tax treatment, allowances, limits and ages reflect our understanding at the time of writing and can change. They are set out to explain how the rules generally work, not to state your personal position. Always check current information from official sources such as GOV.UK, and take advice where appropriate, before acting. Figures across the site are intended to reflect the tax year noted in the footer.

No recommendations or endorsements

InvestAware does not recommend or endorse any provider, platform, product, fund or investment. Where specific things are named — for example, well-known market indexes — they are mentioned purely as examples to explain a concept, never as suggestions to invest.

Independence

We don't sell investments, we don't earn commission for promoting products, and we have no commercial partnerships that influence our content. The aim is education you can trust precisely because there's nothing being sold.

Before you rely on anything here

Treat InvestAware as a starting point for understanding — not as a substitute for regulated advice or official guidance. Final regulatory wording for this site is subject to review.
